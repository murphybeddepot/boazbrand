The VX Manual

How to actually film

The Mechanics

Crouch
Low angles equal power. Phone at knee height or lower.
Lock Focus
Before the trick. Manual focus on the wheels.
No Zoom
Mid-clip. Ever. Crop in editing instead.
Follow Knees
Smooth motion from legs, not arms. Locked elbows.
Get Close
Then closer. The fisheye lies about distance.
Audio
Wheels, slap of the board, ambient sound. Half the magic.

What To Shoot

Lifestyle
Hands fixing griptape. Friends laughing. The drive.
In Between
Brands live in the moments between tricks.
Long Takes
Don't cut every 3 seconds. Let it breathe.
Many Tries
Good clip = 17th attempt, not the 3rd.
Local Spots
Jax/St. Johns is your lookbook. Use it.

Print Specs

Non-negotiable when you call printers

The Specs

Blank
Comfort Colors 1717. Backups: AS Colour Faded, LA Apparel 1801GD.
Method
Discharge printing (preferred) or water-based ink.
Avoid
Plastisol ink. DTG (direct-to-garment).
Palette
Washed seafoam, mustard, faded pepper gray, dusty blue, bone, washed crimson.
First Run
36 shirts. Pay personal $$$, not PEP funds.

The Phone Script

Read it. Memorize it. Dial.
Hey, I'm working on a small brand and putting together a 36-piece run. I want to use Comfort Colors 1717 blanks and I need water-based or discharge ink — not plastisol. Do you handle that, and what's your minimum? Also, which Comfort Colors washes have you had the best discharge results on?
First call will feel awkward. By the third you'll sound like you know what you're doing. Just dial.

Golden Rules

Read every Monday
01
Movement creates clarity.
You'll learn more from 36 flawed shirts than from 6 months of planning the perfect drop. Action beats theory every time.
02
The calls will be awkward.
Script it. Accept the awkwardness. Dial anyway. By the third shop you'll sound like a pro.
03
Document everything.
The paper notebook. The receipts. The printer conversations. In 5 years, win or lose, that notebook will be one of the most valuable things you own.
04
Authenticity beats polish.
Skate and surf culture respect effort and realness over corporate finish. Lo-fi is the point.
05
Permission to walk away.
If a printer pushes plastisol or DTG, a name is taken, or a vendor doesn't take you seriously — walk. The right ones exist.
06
Make 100 people feel seen.
Not 10,000 strangers feel mildly interested. Your audience is Jax Beach kids who recognize the spots. Build for them first.
07
Shoot more than you need.
Always. The good clip is the 17th attempt, not the 3rd.

Mistakes To Avoid

The traps
  • Bella+Canvas blanks — wrong fit, wrong feel
  • Plastisol on vintage designs — kills the illusion
  • Letting shops upsell DTG
  • Generic streetwear names ("Apex," "Rogue," "Echo")
  • Pricing without math (cost × 2.5–4 = retail)
  • Buying a screen printing press (you're a brand-builder)
  • Planning instead of launching
  • Trying to please everyone
